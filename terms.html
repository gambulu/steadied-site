1. What Steadied is

Steadied is a self-help tool for people trying to stop vaping or using nicotine pouches. It tracks how long you have gone without nicotine, records cravings and check-ins you choose to log, shows you patterns in what you have recorded, and offers a short breathing exercise when a craving hits.

2. Steadied is not medical advice

Steadied is not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

The recovery milestones shown in the app describe general patterns commonly reported during nicotine recovery. They are not predictions about your body, and everyone's experience differs. Steadied does not diagnose any condition, does not provide treatment, and does not recommend nicotine replacement products, medications or dosages.

Nicotine withdrawal can affect mood, sleep and concentration, and can interact with existing health conditions and medications. If something feels wrong, or if you are unsure whether stopping is safe for you, talk to a doctor, pharmacist or qualified health professional. Do not delay or disregard professional medical advice because of anything you read in this app.
